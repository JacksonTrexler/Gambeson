## Calls
1. **Deathlust's sign is the existing `Battle.passive()` sign, no HUD.gd edit.** `passive_fired` already lights the slot or floats "Deathlust" over the unit (w7-passives), so no new signal and no log line: `Battle.neutralize()` and `_settle_wear()` now walk `on_passives` (hooks() with the skill each comes from) and sign a passive when its hook lowered its holder's total count (`Exhaustion.total()`) or the use's kept `uses`. Generic: any passive whose `neutralized`/`wearing` hook eases exhaustion signs, none by name. Amount 0 (the sign shows the name only). A kill signs twice (the death eases counts mid-cast, the cancelled use at the cast's end). Alternative: a log line (HUD has `_log`, Battle has none; a sibling edits HUD).
2. **The relief is priced by a dry run of the `neutralized()` hooks** (`AI._relief()`): a blow whose average meets the foe's HP asks u's own passives `neutralized(.., t, u, &"killed")`, reads how far each OTHER skill's extra cost (`_wear()`'s terms) fell, then puts the counts back. Worth = the largest fall (not the sum: it spends its next blow on one skill). Allies' passives are not asked (only u's gain is weighed). Added in `_strike()` after the wear and reprisal discounts, on top of the KILL bonus. Reach is measured from where it stands. Hooks are assumed to touch only the holder's exhaustion (the one place a dry run is restored).
3. **Own plates' tolls** read where it equips: `_dress()` ranks (slot, armor) pairs by `_plated()`, the Defense times the share a block's worth keeps after `Behaviour.slot_toll()` (a blow of `BLOW` 5 saved, the toll x `POINT` paid; none left at a toll of 10). Reads "decides to block" as keeping/putting on a plate (the AI has no other decision where its own plate blocks); a worn plate is valued the same way, so a swap happens only when the net gain is positive. Not built: the slot lapsing as Power runs down (still listed in w5-ai2), and weighing tolls in `_threat()`. With BLOW 5 a Power Armor toll never zeroes a plate (ceil(5/rank) <= 5 x POINT... half at rank I), so the toll chooses between slots, it does not refuse armor.
4. **Crit loops in the forecast**: `Behaviour.Forecast` gains `loops` (follow-ups a crit may set off), `grace` (the chain's free rolls), `rolled` (a follow-up not yet begun: its chain's rolls so far) and `crits()` (the chance a roll of the use crits). `Battle.attack_odds()` takes `rub` (a friction forced, for a follow-up). `AI._use_ratio()` now adds, for k = 1..loops, live x worth of the use again at the friction its chain would have (`rolled = (k-1) x rolls`: the crit taken as the use's last roll), live = the product of each use's crit chance; floored at 0.001. Chaser sets `loops = rank`, `grace = rank` in a `forecasting()` hook (only if something is in reach). Jugular is a skill flag (`recast_on_crit`), not a behaviour, so `_forecast()` reads that generic flag (`loops = RECAST_LIMIT`, `grace = rank`), as `Battle.cast()` does. Calls: a chase is valued as "this use again" (the real chase picks the best of the actives in reach); the chase is free (no AP); `sure_crit` damage doubling is not priced.
5. **A defensive cc read** (`AI._stunners()`, `_dread()`): for each foe it makes out (skills read), the best of its hostile skills by `hit_chance x foresee().afflicts` (CC for a cc tag with no foresee), over 1 + the cc u already bears (`Unit.afflicted()`), with the furthest it could cast from (movement + `skill_range`). `_dread(c)` = the chance at least one lands at tile c within that reach. Used as a tile cost: `DREAD` 4.0 tiles of walking x dread in `_approach()` (it closes from the side out of a Stunner's reach) and a key between the shoves and the gap in `_retreat()` (backs off to a tile out of reach). Insensitive for DREAD 1 to 8 in the sim (swept). Unread skills are not dreaded (as `_shoved`).
   **Tried and dropped: a gate** (hold off, or back off, when closing ends the turn in a cc's reach with no blow). Sim, one Stunner v3 at 7 or 8 tiles: stuns taken 1.88 -> 2.25, wins 2 -> 1 of 8: the foe just walks up and stuns it, and against a sentry the unit would never fight. Tile choice cannot stall and costs nothing when every tile is covered.
