# Unbounded maps, camera and spawning (z-maps, 2026-11-16)

Task: the queue card "Unbounded maps, camera and spawning" (plan.md Phase 7 item 27). The Meadows
already streamed 12-tile chunks around the party, the camera already kept to the player's unit, and
demons already spawned beyond perception through `summon()`. What was missing: work that grows with the
map (whole-map loops), a camera you can move, and spawns checked by the real sight rules. The 30-kill
portal stays as it is (a later battle).

## Readings and calls (for review)

1. **Streaming stays the Meadows'.** Chunks are generated by `Meadows.ensure_around()` from its biome
   noise; I didn't lift a generic "streamer" out of it (only one map streams; over-abstracting). What
   changed is what triggers it: any party move (`traveled`), plus a forced move (`driven`) - a shove off
   the edge of the known plane now grows it instead of stopping at the edge.
2. **Bounded work: one helper, `Battle.cells_near(c, r)`.** The on-map cells within r tiles of c,
   walking only the box around c (clipped to the used rect). Every "for c in map.get_used_cells() if
   distance <= r" loop goes through it: Luun's and Vetruvis's prayers, an explosion's exposure,
   `strike_tiles()`, `free_near()` (rings outwards), `drag_spot()` (the caster's movement), `_scatter()`
   (around the path), and the AI's Observe and channel candidate cells. A source test keeps new
   `get_used_cells()` loops out of Battle and AI (the Meadows' known culprit).
3. **Suspicion is bounded by sight.** `maybe_suspicious(o)` read every concealing tile of the plane
   (every brush tile) for every unit every turn. Where sight is limited (only the Meadows), a tile
   beyond o's perception range can't look suspicious to it: its candidates are its sight's box, not
   the map. A rule change, only where `Battle.sight` is on: a hidden trap 20 tiles off no longer gets a
   "disturbed earth?" hint for a unit that perceives 12. Where sight isn't limited nothing changes.
4. **Camera.** Arrow keys pan (four new actions, `camera_left/right/up/down`, rebindable in KeyBinds
   like the rest). Panning lets go of the unit; the view keeps to the active unit again when someone
   acts, the turn changes, the selection changes, or Home. "The active unit": the one the player
   selects, or the last unit to act that the player's side makes out (Read: located) - a demon moving
   unseen doesn't pull the view to itself (that would give it away). No edge-pan or drag: the HUD's
   panels sit on the edges and the mouse buttons are taken (left, right, middle: look-over).
5. **Spawning unseen, in `summon()`.** `summon(scene, f, c, unseen_by := null)`: with a side given,
   the unit lands on the nearest free tile to c where no unit of that side would make it out - the real
   read (`Battle.read()`: perception range, concealment, the unit's own hiding), not a distance - or
   isn't summoned (null). Meadows' spawns pass the party's side; its own distance check stays only as
   the cheap first guess for where to look. Where sight isn't limited every unit is seen, so the check
   is skipped (spawning would otherwise be impossible) - a call.
