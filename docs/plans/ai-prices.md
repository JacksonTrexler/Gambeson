# AI prices what it ignores (z-aiprice, 2026-11-15)

Branch `z-aiprice`. Items from `docs/todo/systems.md` (?138, ?150), cards in `docs/queue.md`. Baseline: 8070+ checks; after: 8289 checks, 0 failed (873 tests; combat_test's numbers moved with the new weights, as expected).

## Calls (each open for review)

1. **Defense from Read.** `Read.defense` (null unless its armor shows: net 0 up, the HUD's own rule, which now reads it: `HUD._defense_shows` = `known(u) or view(u).defense != null`). `Battle.hit_chance(..., guess := -1)`: a guess >= 0 stands in for the target's Defense; `AI._chance()` passes **GUESS_DEFENSE = 12** (a medium armor's floor) when the read has none. A seen Defense is the true one (as the player's odds). Not done: Defense through a lie (`_lie`/`twist`), as the HUD doesn't either.
2. **Pricing the rest, no per-skill branch in AI.gd.**
   - Cost, not only AP: `score()`'s `saved` is `share x (AP cost x AP + cost points x POINT)`; **POINT = 0.5** HP per Energy/Focus point (a guess: a strike's cost is a few points). Poach's half price now counts its Energy.
   - `Behaviour.foresee` gains two optional keys, summed by `Battle.foresee()`: `riders` (expected count of riders; default one per note) and `spill` (net HP others take: foes' less the caster's friends'). The AI adds `RIDER x riders + spill` to a blow's value (so x hit chance).
   - Poach: a blow whose average fells the target (`damage_range` avg >= hp) is one rider (regain, `felled()`), with a note. Jam: the interrupt is one rider; Slam Jam: a rider at its contest's odds (`Battle.odds`, as `apply()` rolls it), the note says the percent. *Calls:* interrupt worth a flat RIDER (1.0) whatever the target is doing (no extra for a channel to end); regain worth a flat RIDER whatever it restores.
3. **Kick.** `Kick.driven()` reads a push to where it stops (map edge or unit, as `displace`): the note says the tiles really driven, a stop short adds the slam (`Battle.SLAM` per tile) to the target's lo/hi and, as spill, to the unit struck (negative for an ally of the kicker). Tiles are riders at **PUSH = 0.5** each. A trap's blast: each other unit within `blast` of the trap (kicker too, if it stands there) takes the dice mean as spill (foe +, own -), noted "Its blast catches N foes and M of its own". No mitigation, boost or reactions counted.
4. **Grudge.** `_starve` no longer needs the Grudge unit to be the last foe. `starve_worth(b, u, g)` = **STARVE = 5.0** (a kill) / the number of units of u's side in g's reach (movement + 1, itself included): each would have to keep out; **0** once g dies of its rounds anyway (`Downed.State.left <= 1`). It starves when that beats `_best_strike()`, the best `score()` u has on any other standing foe in reach (0 with none: the old last-foe case is unchanged). Several rounds: the turn is judged afresh each round (so it keeps it up as long as g lingers), and a Grudge unit on its last round is left alone. Not done: planning a retreat tile so as not to be cornered next round; the allies don't coordinate (each AI weighs its own share).
5. **Final Act.** A second new generic hook, `Behaviour.reprisal(r, holder, rank)`: HP the holder's reaction would cost the blow's author, asked at the same moments as `foils` (`AI._moments()` now builds them once for both). Final Act: its best strike at the author, hit chance x average damage. `_strike` takes the cost off the blow's worth but never below `1 - FOILED` of it, so a lone Final Act unit is finished, left for last. Replaces Final Act's `foils` share (WORTH 0.25, removed).
