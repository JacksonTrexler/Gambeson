# w2-glance: Glance, Backstab, Glancing Strike, Heavy Strike

The user's words (2026-11-18, verbatim):
"Rogue / Slayer martial t4 skill: backstab: gives rank critical hit ease (may miss 20 by rank and still crit). aside: critical hits on a miss `glance` instead. `glance` half regular hit damage, but a hit regardless.
general martial t2: Glancing Strike: Strikes are rolled at disadvantage. If both rolls don't miss though, strike hits with `glance`. Add rank to the first rank attack rolls made.
general t5 martial: Heavy Strike: You deal consecutive strike damage as a single attack (attacks rolled as usual). Unit must pass a final attack check, adding successes + rank to attack roll, or it misses instead. On a hit, add successes + rank damage."

## Readings and calls (for review)

1. **Glance** (the user's word, no ??): an attack face in crit range that would otherwise miss is a glance, a hit for half the regular damage, rounded down, at least 1. A natural 20 no longer always crits: it crits on a hit and glances on a miss (before, a natural 20 always hit and crit). Effects, behaviours and riders land on a glance in full (it is a hit); only damage halves.
2. Outcome is decided in one place, `Check.outcome()` (MISS / HIT / CRIT / GLANCE), read by `Battle.resolve()` and by `Check.outcomes()` (the unrolled forecast). The payload is `Check.Roll` (`hit`, `crit`, `glance`, `other` die, `against()` to re-judge it on a swapped target), announced by a new signal `Battle.attack_rolled(caster, target, roll)` beside `attacked` (kept unchanged).
3. **Crit range hook**: a new Effect stat `crit_range` (title "Crit range ??"): faces below 20 that are in crit range; `Battle.crit_range(u)` adds it to Discipline's points; never below 2 (the existing `keen()` floor). Any status, item or passive can carry it.
4. **Backstab**: passive, `crit_range` 1 per rank (rank I: 19 and up). Class roots have no tiers, so "t4" can't be set: on Slayer it is after Jugular (the crit skill), on the Rogue's Blade root after Jam. Both readings of where "t4" sits are calls.
5. **Glancing Strike** (general martial tier 2, passive, not a Strike form, so it stacks with Brutal/Heavy): two Effect stats. `glancing` ("Glancing ??"): the holder's Strikes (skills with `chain`; there is no strike tag) roll at disadvantage, and *any* of its attacks rolled at disadvantage (Gank, Jugular, Strikes) whose better die would land (hit or glance) is a glance instead of a miss. Reading of "if both rolls don't miss": the worse die misses but the better would not. Advantage from elsewhere cancels the disadvantage as usual (the glance rule then doesn't apply). The disadvantage is a cost; no balancing.
6. `opening_rolls` ("Opening attacks ??"): + rank to hit on each of the holder's first rank attack rolls each battle, counted by a new generic counter `Unit.rolls_made` (every attack roll through `Battle.roll_attack()`, Heavy Strike's last roll included). No such per-battle counter existed (`Unit.tally` clears each turn).
7. **Heavy Strike** (general martial tier 5, passive, a Strike form: exclusive `strike_form`, renames Strike "Heavy Strike", so not with Brutal/Decisive/Elemental). "Consecutive strikes" = one Strike use's hits (rank hits, Strike Out's misses that don't end it, Strike!'s restrikes). "Successes" = hits that landed. Each hit's damage (its row penalty, crit or glance already applied) is held, then a last attack: the last hit's bonus without its row penalty + successes + rank, against the current Defense and edge; a miss loses everything and the Strike counts as a miss (`_miss`, no landed hits); a hit deals pool + successes + rank, doubled on a critical, halved on a glance. No hits landed: no last roll.
8. Heavy Strike is a behaviour on a new generic hook, `Behaviour.striking(b, holder, rank, v: Volley)`: a Strike's payload per landing hit (`dealt` mutable) and once at the end (`dealt` lands as one blow; `missed`). Other passives can join it.
9. Jugular's "a hit is a critical" leaves a glance a glance; its recast reads a hit in crit range (as it did with Discipline).
10. Forecast: `Battle.hit_chance()` now reads `attack_odds()` ([hit, crit, glance], exact via `Check.outcomes()`), built by the same `attack_check()` the roll uses, so crit range, glancing and opening rolls show. The HUD's hover line adds "(N% a glance)"; the log says "(glance)" / "(critical)" and floats "Glance". The AI's damage is cut by half the glance share. Crits still add nothing to expected damage (as before).
11. Glossary: new term `glance` ("Glance") and the Critical/Attack definitions updated; colour `#e6c98a`.
12. `Util/skill.py`: `striking` added to its list of hooks (a sibling also edits Util/).
