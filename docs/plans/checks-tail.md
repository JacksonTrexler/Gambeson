# Checks tail and rebate affordability (z-checkstail, 2026-11-15)

Starting suite: 8391 checks, 0 failed (882 tests). Final (after merging adventurer-skills with x-equip and z-aiprice): 8744 checks, 0 failed (897 tests). No existing test moved.

## Readings
- Level Down's bar is `max(average mod + level, last_stand(t))`: the last stand is *rolled once* per cast and shared by all `rank` rolls, so the chance is not rank independent rolls of one number.
- Investigating: the hider rolls one guise; every watcher looks against that same total.
- Only Invisibility among the hiding skills is allied-targetable, so the AI's `_support` is the only caster of an investigated skill.
- Hitch and Slam Jam's check follows a hit; `Skill.check` stands in place of the roll and is read in cast(), hit_chance(), HUD brackets, contests(), the AI.

## Calls
1. **Forecast through the helper.** New `Check.totals(c)` (each total a side may roll, with its chance). `Battle.level_odds()` weighs each last-stand total, then `1 - (1-p)^rank` of `Battle.odds(.., against = bar)` (chance of at least one level); `hit_chance()` answers it, so brackets, Alt's "N% to win" (HUD `_contests`) and `AI.score()` read it. `Battle.seen_odds()` does the same over the hider's guise totals (`Battle.guise()` is now what cast() rolls) and each watcher's `odds(.., against = total)`. HUD: Alt tooltip over the unit a hiding skill is used on, "N% to be seen through", only counting foes the player can make out. AI: `_support` skips an investigated skill foes see through at least `AI.SEEN` (0.9) of the time. That AI rule is mine: change if unwanted. Death's chance is not forecast (not asked).
2. **Fixed-DC rolls** on `check(.., against = dc)`: Burnout's con save, Stun, Bandwagon, glass's dex check, `evade`, pick/pry (`free_from`), the held-fire wis check. rng order unchanged (a fixed side rolls nothing). Left: the initiative d20 and the two last-stand rolls (no DC). Stun says its chance as a rider (`foresee`, "Stuns it (N%)", `riders = p`).
3. **Hitch and Slam Jam are NOT a declared `SkillCheck`.** `Skill.check` is read as "in place of the attack roll" in cast, hit_chance, the HUD brackets, `contests()`, the AI; "after a hit" would be a flag every reader branches on, plus a foe-wins-ties field and an array `against` (Hitch rolls the better of str/dex). Not worth three fields on a shared resource. Instead each keeps one static options function (`HITCH.grapple()`, `SLAM_JAM.contest()`) that `apply()` rolls and `foresee()` weighs, so the forecast cannot drift from the roll. Both say their chance as a rider with `riders = p` (the hook z-aiprice added; Slam Jam's was merged into mine).
4. **Affordability.** `Unit.can_afford(s, cut)`, `can_afford_after(s, steps, cut)`, `funding(s, cut)` (the fund chosen is for the discounted price; `pay_for` asks the same). `Battle.price_cut(caster, skill, targets)` = the smallest sure share of the targets (a price paid once for a spread must be granted by all; none to ask: 0), `Battle.can_afford(caster, skill, target, steps)`. Used by the HUD (`why_not`, `_approach` reach and readiness, drain's fund) and the AI (`_strike`, `_could_strike`, `_steady`, `_starve`; those three no longer prefilter with `foe_actives(true)`, which is now unused). Auto casts (min over `auto_targets`) and tile casts (the unit on the tile, if any) pay with the cut. Slot-press checks with no target (`select`) keep the full price.

## Tests
- `poach_test.test_afford_the_cut`: half the AP, hale and bloodied: Unit, Battle, the HUD's reason and walk readiness, `AI._strike`, price_cut over several targets, auto and tile payment.
- `last_stand_test.test_level_down_odds`: forecast vs 1500 casts; the shared bar is not rank copies of its mean.
- `bracing_test.test_seen_odds`: one watcher is the contest's odds; two are more but not chained; shared guise vs 1500 rolls.
- `check_test.test_dc_and_rider_forecasts`: no inline `Check.check(` outside the helper (Battle: 3 left), a dodge as often as the odds (with a defensive-only bonus), Stun/Hitch/Slam Jam's note and riders = the odds, Stun lands as forecast.
- `hud_test.test_forecast_bar_and_sight`, `ai_support_test.test_hides_where_unseen`.
- Mutations (each failed its test, then reverted): auto payment without the cut; tile payment without it; AI `_strike` with plain can_afford; HUD why_not; HUD walk readiness; Unit's ap_cost without the cut; price_cut min -> max; level odds without the last stand; level odds not raised to rank; dodge role dropped; HUD `_contests` without Level Down; `seen_odds` max instead of chain. **Unproven:** Hitch's apply() options (only foresee's are tested); `hit_chance`'s Level Down branch alone (level_odds is tested directly, the HUD bracket only needs a non-empty answer).
