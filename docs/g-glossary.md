## Center

center: the largest city within the region / the previous capital of lancin now fallen to criminal activity. decades have since past and Lancela over in Lancin Proper is the new font of power for the faction. Their official story is Center should effectivley be considered a recently revassalized city state that still needs time to acclimate, though they have no formal control over the region past neither the organized criminals of the outer rings and lower reaches or Lancin's republic leadership standing to benefit from all-out conflict.

- Lancin: the faction (a republic; Center was its capital).
- Lancela: the city in Lancin Proper that is now its seat of power.
- Lancin Proper: the part of Lancin outside Center's region.
