# Done: systems

`[x]` done · `(?N)` an open question in `docs/todo/` that may still change it · *Now:* what the game does today. Index: `completed.md`.

- [x] **Attack rolls.** d20 + attack mod against Defense for the skill's tags; a natural 20 hits and doubles damage; at least 1 damage before mitigation. The forecast shows the chance to hit when the player can identify the target, and the log shows each roll. A skill's `rolls` switch (in the editor) turns its roll off. (?5)
- [x] **Defense.** Armor sets it; `defense` effects add to it, untagged or tagged, with child tags counting (`physical` covers `pierce`). The unit card shows it ("11 · Gambeson", plus any tagged extras). Still to come: Sundered. Attack Down (Interdict) cuts the attacker's damage dealt instead.
- [x] **Skill trees and ranks.** Roots (`SkillRoot`) of branches (`Branch`: the skills a branch comes after, or a count of its root's skills for capstones), a class's centre and core, and stubs for skills waiting on a question. Ranking up costs 1 + r², from the skills screen; a skill's `max_rank` can cap it. Tiers (`Role.tier()`) show on the skills screen. *Now:* only the Adventurer has a tree; other classes list their options, which can be ranked up too.
- [x] **Tabbed skill trees**: a tab per path (`SkillTab`: a centre, core and roots, like a class's own tree; `Role.tabs`). The skills screen shows a tab's wheel at a time, with a button per tab where the title was. A class's starting skills show on whichever tab has them. *Now:* the Rogue and Bard each have their own tab and **Calling: Freedom**.
- [x] **Energy recovery.** Rest, light activity and busy turns, as in the rule. A float shows what's regained.
- [x] **Dual-target skills**: buff allies, hurt enemies (starter spells). A skill's `dual` switch: allies get its effects, foes its damage, after an attack roll.
- [x] **Dragging** (a skill's `drags_to`; `Battle.drag_spot()`, `Battle.drag()`): a contest in place of the attack roll (`Battle.contested`, logged "(14 - 11)"); won, the foe goes onto the nearest free ground with the tag that the caster could walk to, and the caster in beside it. Drown. (?14)
- [x] **Grapples** (a skill's `grip`; `Battle._break_free()`, `Battle._grip()`): the foe is held (`grappled`) until it breaks free. Moving away first costs 1 + the holder's mod in the grip's stat tiles of movement and a contest in that stat (the holder keeps it on a tie); won, it walks on with what's left. Each turn it ends held, it takes the grip's dice + the holder's mod. A holder that falls or leaves its side lets go. Drown: 1d12 + str mod.
- [x] **Investigation**: enemies that can see a unit use a deceptive skill (Hide, Clever Disguise) check it at once, at advantage when close, against the one roll it made (kept on its effects; less its dissonance). Those who pass see through it. A skill's `investigated` switch turns this on.
- [x] **Seeing rolls** (Self-evaluation): a roll an ally's Self-evaluation reaches tallies up above the unit, like the initiative dice, and labels its effect's icon on the card. (?20)
- [x] **Standing still**: what counts as not having moved (resting, concealment that lasts until a unit moves, skills). Meander stretches it to rank tiles a turn. A skill's `until_move` switch makes its effects last until the unit moves.
- [x] **Fading effects**: hold for some turns, then lose a level a turn (a skill's `fades` switch: Clever Disguise).
- [x] **Per-skill rank costs**: a skill can scale its rank costs its own way (`rank_cost`, `rank_growth`: Clever Disguise).
- [x] **Self-only skills** (`self_only`: Hide, Clever Disguise): used from the hotbar at once, on the unit itself.
- [x] **Automatic skills** (a skill's `auto`; `Battle.auto_targets()`): used from the hotbar at once, landing on the nearest units in range the caster can place, allies and foes alike, no more than its rank; paid once. Sing.
- [x] **Dissonance and insight** (see Persona and insight): `Unit.dissonance()`, a point for each thing its persona puts forward at its rank (each vibe word; its stance and a claimed level; each identity tag and its name), one each for Embellish and Brood, plus `extra_dissonance` (Unit → Persona). The read sits on the unit card under its rows. Dissonance comes off the unit's own deceiving checks (`Unit.dissonance_penalty()`): the roll a deceiving skill makes, and the check it makes against units looking harder at it. A skill carrying its look cuts that by its rank (the Siren's Alluring IV: 7 − 4 = 3); insight still reads the full tally.
- [x] **Persona looks** (a skill's Look group; `Unit._take_looks()`): a stance, vibe words, tags and name pools a skill puts forward on whoever has it, on top of the unit's own, as the unit sets up. Alluring.
- [x] **Perceived stats**: the unit card shows a unit's **BST** (its six core stats added up) when its stats can be made out (net 1). Embellish inflates it and Brood deflates it, for those they fool.
- [x] **Fronting without trickery**: persona, Embellish and Brood fool allies that aren't insightful (`Perception.fronts_to()`). Allies fooled, by fronting or trickery, see the presentation, never the twisted fields.
- [x] **Stances** (the wrong team): `stance` on the unit (Unit → Persona), shown to those it fools from Persona III (Friendly from II). Reads carry it (`Read.stance`), and `Read.attitude()` says how the reader takes it: ally, foe, avoid or ignore. The AI strikes only foes, keeps a tile clear of the aggressive, and supports only allies. The unit card shows Aggressive, Passive or Traitor in place of Friendly or Enemy. Chosen in play with Personify's modal.
- [x] **Teammates**: the leader orders, and takes into its party, only units that it and they each take for a teammate (`Battle.teammates()`); a Traitor pretends to.
- [x] **Traitors in the enemy's party** (`Battle.posing()`): a Traitor that fools a leader can join its party. It acts on the leader's turn too, on whatever AP its own turn left it, and the player orders it like any party member (it shows only as the side reads it). Told to turn a hostile skill on its real allies, it refuses, or with a positive cha mod misses on purpose; either way the leader looks harder at it (`Battle.doubt()`: at advantage for a refusal, at disadvantage for a whiff). Not resting on its own side's turn counts as a refusal (`Battle.refused`, logged). Not ordered, it keeps beside its leader; on its own turn it rests unless it can make a kill (`AI._keep_up()`). Seen through, it leaves the party as the leader's next turn starts.
- [x] **Reading allies** by wis mod (`Perception._dim()`): −1 to −3, no details but healthy or bloodied and a word or two (`Perception.traits()`); −4 or less, only its side and what it looks like (`Role.noun`). Persona advertising (HP and level from II, vibe from I, identity from III) still shows. A unit's own concealment doesn't hide it from its allies.
- [x] **No cheating**: the unit card and tooltips show everyone but the leader as the player's side reads them (`HUD.view()`: the clearest read of its other units), its own allies included. They still show AP and movement while taking orders. An ally's skills show by name only (`Read.kit`) until net 3, and as "?" to a leader of negative wis mod, which can't order them used (`HUD.skill_sense()`). Its effects show as a "?" at net 1, in full at net 3.
- [x] **Gullible**: wis mod −3 or less falls for any front, an enemy's too, without trickery (`Perception.fronts_to()`).
- [x] **Advertised level** (`shows_level`, Persona II): its own, or one it claims to those it fools.
- [x] **Ramut's Ire** (`Battle._check_stall()`, `Skills/ramuts_ire.tres`, the `ire` stat): a side's turn is stalled when none of its units makes out a foe it would fight while enemies stand; the foes hiding or posing stall it. A turn that isn't stalled starts that side's count over. Once more turns in a row have stalled than the grace (`Battle.grace()`: the units remaining on the smallest side, × `ire_grace` (< 0: never), less what the side's prayers to Ramut took off), the Ire builds a rank on each of them every stalled turn, logged. Each rank defeats a rank of its concealment and trickery, against everyone, for the rest of the battle.
- [x] **Forced movement**, a pull (`Battle.pull()`, a skill's `pull`): Sing. Dragging is above; pushes are still to come.
- [x] **Craven** (`behaviour` on the unit): won't advance without an ally it trusts within 3 tiles whose level it reads as higher (`AI.CRAVEN_REACH`). Backs away from units it takes for Aggressive within that reach (`AI._flee()`).
- [x] **Lure** (`behaviour`: the Siren): serene and still until unmasked (`AI._luring()`). What comes into its reach it drowns, then rakes (`AI._ambush()`), leaving what it already holds to its grip; with nothing in reach, it sings.
- [x] **Healing** (a skill's Heal group; `Battle.mend()`): HP from the caster's mods, wounds cleared up to the heal's rank (`Effect.wound`; Renew: rank × rank), AP back (Renew), and per_wound × rank more for each wound cleared (First Aid). Regen clears wounds up to its own rank as it heals. Traps can leave wounds (`Terrain.wounds`: Snare's Bleeding, the `bleed` stat: dice + the setter's mod as each of the afflicted unit's side's turns ends, except the turn it was put on).
- [x] **Healer behaviours** (`AI._heal()`, `AI.want()`): how a unit tends (`Unit.tends`, else `Role.tends`): benevolent (the default; the largest share of HP missing), pragmatic (the highest level it reads, unless over half the heal would go to waste), loyal (its party first: `Unit.follows`, set by joining a party). Seen wounds it can clear count; commanders (the player's leader, or one with a party) a little more.
- [x] **Suspicion** (`Unit.suspicion`, `Battle.suspect()`, `Battle.doubt()`): stacks a unit builds on an enemy or a tile, each defeating a rank of its trickery and concealment (a tile's: what hides there) for that unit alone; any suspicion of a unit breaks its front. Units an enemy they can place is fooling look harder as their turn starts, and when it damages them with a hostile skill. Shown as a **Su** icon on the card, where the side makes out effects.
- [x] **Evidence** (`Battle._scatter()`, `Battle.debris`): using a skill that puts concealment on a unit (Hide) scatters debris from its investigated roll (`LITTER`): 15 or more (`CLEAN_HIDE`) leaves 1–2, 2–4 tiles off; 10–14 (`FAIR_HIDE`) 2–3, 1–3 off; less, 3–4 within 2 tiles. Moving while still concealed scatters one fewer around the path (not on it), by the roll its concealment was put on with, else d20 + dex mod (`Battle._hide_roll()`). Stepping on debris destroys it; `Battle._tidy_debris()` (after moves, casts and each side's turn) clears a revealed hider's (no concealment left, or fallen) and any whose hider is further than its band's far edge + dex mod. Kinds by the tile's tags, then `Battle.area_tags` (`DEBRIS_KINDS`); tiles with none (water) get none. Terrain tags added for it: grass (Grass, and the Spike Trap that looks like it), shrubbery (Bramble), stone (Rough, Wet Rock, Shrine); these material tags aren't listed in the tile panel. Enemies that make out the ground see it (`Battle.evidence()`; drawn as scattered strokes, with a tile-panel line), and the AI's Observe counts it as a clue.
- [x] **Hard to read** (`Perception._dim()`): a reader's wis mod for an ally's read is less the ally's cha mod when it's negative (a positive one adds nothing). Villagers (wis 12, cha 8) still read each other in full.
- [x] **Inspecting** (`Battle.inspect()`): a suspicious tile is one where a unit makes out something but not what (a hint of a hidden trap or unit), or one it already suspects and still can't make out. Each unit inspects the nearest one it hasn't before as its turn starts and after it moves. As its side's turn ends it looks around (`Battle._watch()`, `Battle.survey()`): the nearest within max(wis mod, 1) + rested tiles, 1 + rested of them, at +rested (rested: its turns in a row resting, up to its wis); with wis mod 0 or less only once every (1 − wis mod) turns; Eyes Forward, when it fires, replaces this look. Investigation skills search every tile in their area instead (`Battle.search()`). A tile where nothing hides gets no roll. Each check is d20 + wis mod + bonus − the distance penalty (`Battle.distance_penalty()`: none for adjacent tiles, else the tiles from the centre), against d20 + the concealment there. Passing grows a stack on the tile, and on a hidden enemy standing there. Logged for the player's units.
- [x] **Resisted skills** (a skill's `resisted`; `Battle._lured()`): only an enraptured foe (convinced the caster is its ally, with no suspicion of it) is pulled. On a suspicious one, the caster's stat against its `resisted` stat takes a stack of suspicion off. Sing: cha against wis.
- [x] **Grappler AI** (`AI._rooted()`): holding a unit, a unit won't walk off and let go, unless it reads its own tile as bad ground.
- [x] **Log grammar** (see Design rules): skill uses, rolls, investigations, suspicion, grips, refusals, traps and the Ire follow it.
- [x] **Disabled skills** (`Unit.disabled`, `Unit.disable()`): a skill switched off for some of its holder's turns can't be used, and a disabled passive stops working; they count down with its effects. Allies still read them in its kit. The hotbar dims a disabled skill, its tooltip saying for how long; one a curse disabled (`Unit.cursed`: Brainfog) also quivers gently (`SkillSlot.cursed`).
- [x] **Pillar skills** (`pillar` tag): what a build stands on (Powerful, Physical Intelligence). Disabling one disables all of them. A disabling skill reaches them first, as one slot (`Unit.disable_order()`): they sit in the last sure slots. Then come the always-on passives (filling the bar from the bottom), then the hotbar from its last slot back, then any other actives. Against the check, the pillar slot weighs its median rank (`Unit.slot_weight()`). Pillar skills that go on the hotbar take its last sure slots and can't be moved or covered (`Unit.bar_place()`, `Unit.bar_clear()`, `Unit._fill_bar()`); everything else can be reordered.
- [x] **Level effects** (the `level` stat; `Unit.lv()`): a buff or curse moves a unit's level for a while. Sure slots go by the higher of its level and its level now, so a buff adds slots (gone, skills and all, as it ends) and a curse's lost slots stay on the bar sealed (`Unit.sealed()`: unusable, dim and quivering). The clover slot, armor's level rule and how others read its level go by its level now.
- [x] **Skill attributes** (`Unit.skill_stat()`, `Skill.query()`): effects on a skill's attributes, asked with its tags (plus `damage`, for a skill with damage tiers) and narrowed to named skills by `Effect.skills`. `range` adds to its range (`Skill.span()`); `rank` to its rank as used (`Unit.rank()`; `Unit.base_rank()` is what's learned and raised). `opening_ap` gives more general AP on a unit's first turn of a battle (`Unit.turns_taken`). Item effects scale by the item's rank.
- [x] **Shared tier 1 row** (`Role.shared`): skills on every tab's core ring, tier 1, learned from the start as the class's innate skills. A class's own tab is left out when it would be empty beside other tabs. The Warlock: a tab per patron.
- [x] **Reserves from passives** (`Skill.reserve`, `Skill.reserve_mod`; `Unit.pools()`): a passive can give its holder a reserve, its max the holder's mod × the passive's rank, at least 0. Reserves can pay a skill's whole cost with a few points (`Reserve.whole`), come back for each foe their holder slays (`Reserve.on_kill`) or as its patron is satisfied (`Reserve.patron`; `Unit.gain()`). A skill can need a reserve (`Skill.needs`): nothing else pays for it. Sweet Nothings.
- [x] **Effects that climb and fall** (`Effect.arc`; `Unit.Applied.strength()`): an amount that's a step, climbing a step a turn to the middle of its turns and back down (5 turns: 1, 2, 3, 2, 1). wauaw. A timed effect's strength also has a `scale`, for Effect strength set when cast.
- [x] **Damage dealt** (the `damage` stat): that fraction more damage dealt with skills. wauaw.
- [x] **Either-side skills** (`Skill.either`): usable on allies and foes alike, its effects on both, without an attack roll. wauaw.
- [x] **Coordinate's pooled reads** (`Battle.read()`, `Battle.linked()`, the `coordinate` stat): units the same Coordinate linked each make out what the best of them does, up to the link's rank in levels better than they would alone (units and tiles).
- [x] **Items, more** (`Item`): a trinket slot (`Unit.trinket`); effects on the wearer (`Item.effects`, as passives); a `max_hp` effect (`Unit.refit()`: Burning Blade); items that need a positive mod to wear (`Item.requires`); hearts while worn (`Item.hearts`, `Item.hearts_mod`); Defense from hearts spent (`spent_defense`; armor that sets no Defense leaves it unarmored: `Item.sets_defense()`); Power back on a melee hit (`refills`); and consumables (`slot` consumable: a Heart). `Unit.equip()` / `unequip()`.
- [x] **Hearts** (`Unit.hearts`, `Unit.lives()`, `Unit.hearts_used`; `Battle._on_downed()`, `Battle._revive()`): taking mortal damage with a heart left, a unit goes down instead of falling: it holds its tile, can't be targeted, and loses its effects, disabled skills and channel. It gets up at full HP as the current side's turn ends (`Battle.revived`), bringing what it summons (`Unit.summons`). A side's lives are its units' (`Battle.lives()`, `Battle.lives_left()`). Logged ("Astral Princess is down, a heart spent (1 left)"); a downed unit shows faded, with a Down chip and its hearts on its card. (?29)
- [x] **Summoning** (`Battle.summon()`): a unit brought onto the free tile nearest a cell, on a side, mid-battle. The HUD follows it.
- [x] **Regeneration Pulse** (the `pulse` stat; `Battle._pulse()`): as its side's turn starts, once every (its side's lives left, at least 1) of them, it heals each ally within 3 tiles (`PULSE_REACH`), itself too, (1d4 + 5% of their max HP) × rank. Logged. (?29)
- [x] **Power** (the `powered` stat; `Unit.powered()`): Energy that never comes back on its own: resting gives nothing; Starblade gives it back. The bar is yellow tinted orange, pulsing white above 75% and blue when full; cards label it PW. (?29)
- [x] **Energy damage** (skills tagged `energy`; `Unit.drain()`): what gets through mitigation drains Energy (or Power) first; then `carry` × (rank − 1) of the rest goes on to HP. Smite.
- [x] **Channeled skills** (`Skill.channel`; `Battle.cast_at()`, `Battle._detonate()`, `Unit.Channel`): cast on a tile in range, paying AP and cost, the caster channels it there. Cast again for its strike AP and cost: a tile within (its side's turns since) tiles of where it was cast explodes, hitting everyone within `blast` × rank tiles but the caster. The channel ends on striking, or when the caster goes down or falls. The HUD says why a tile can't be struck. Metea. (?29)
- [x] **Drops and spoils** (`Unit.loot`, `Loot`; `Battle.spoils`, `Battle.winnings()`, `Battle.rewards`): a unit may drop an item as it falls (weighted, with a chance), logged. The side that wins takes home what other sides dropped, plus the scenario's rewards; the victory screen lists them, and they go to the stash (`Game.stash`).
- [x] **Equipping items** (the character sheet, `CharacterSheet`): the dock's character icon opens the selected unit's sheet (else the leader's): its weapon, armor and trinket, its hearts, and the stash. On its side's turn, or once the battle is over, a unit of the player's own puts items on (what they replace goes back in the stash), takes them off, or uses up a consumable or a potion in its trinket slot (`Battle.use_item()`, `Battle.restore()`). In battle each costs the item's AP (`Battle.equip()`, `Battle.unequip()`, `Battle.equip_ap()`; `Item.ap_cost()`), shown on the sheet's buttons. A new starter wears its class's items (`Role.items`, set by the entry screen), and keeps its outfit and hearts for the next battle (`Game.outfit`, `Game.hearts`).
- [x] **The player's view** (`HUD.view()`, `HUD.tile_view()`): other sides show as the player's unit (the leader, else the first of its side still standing: `HUD.observer()`) makes them out, through `Battle.read()` - so Coordinate's pooling is how the rest of a side's sight reaches it.
- [x] **Gods' damage** (`Unit.GOD_TAGS`, `Skill.god_tag`, `Battle.hit_tags()`): a skill can carry the caster's god's damage tag (Smite).
- [x] **Devotion** (`SkillTab.devotes`, `Unit.devotion()`): learning a skill on a devoting tab's roots closes the others (a Warlock's patrons). **Stat swaps** (`Role.swaps`): a class's magical skills use one stat for another (Warlock: cha for wis).
- [x] **Uncontrolled allies** (`Unit.uncontrolled`): on the player's side, but they take no orders and join no party; they act on their own as the turn ends. Shades.
- [x] **Joining the player's side** (`Unit.with_player`): a scenario's units that take whichever side the player's starter is on.
- [x] **Persona descriptors** (`Unit.intention`, `Unit.strategy`; `Read.intention`, `Read.strategy`): what a unique unit means to do and how it picks targets. An insightful observer reads them at net 2; they show under its card's insight line. `strategy` drives its AI: `strongest` (or `weakest`) weighs the level it reads, half a tile a level, against distance (`AI._best_target()`). (?29)
- [x] **Skittish** (`behaviour`: a Regeneration Probe): it only keeps away from its foes (`AI._keep_away()`, sharing `AI._away()` with craven units).
- [x] **Channeling AI** (`AI._channel()`): holding a channel, a unit strikes the tile it can that takes in the most foes it can place, less its allies; not yet channeling, it begins where a blast (a tile wider, for their moving) would take in the most.
- [x] **Scenarios** on the entry screen (`Entry.scenarios`): the Museum, or the Astral Envoy.
- [x] **The star lattice** (`Battle.panes`, `Battle.starpower`, `Battle.charge`; `star_source()`, `star_rate()`, `_starlight()`): starpanes and starpower sources set per battle; Power back on the lattice as its side's turn ends, by the fullest source it's joined to; a blunt blow on an empty pane breaks it (`Battle.attempt()`, `shattered`). Drawn by `Overlay._lattice()`.
- [x] **Weapon damage tags** (`Item.damage_tag`, `Skill.weapon_tag`, `Battle.hit_tags()`): a skill can deal its wielder's weapon's kind of blow, blunt bare-handed (Strike).
- [x] **Starsap, Starkind and reforming** (`sap`, `starkind`, `reforming` stats; `Battle._sap()`, `Battle._starkind()`; `Unit.reverts_to`, `Unit.reforms_into`; `reverted`, `reformed`).
- [x] **Neutral sides** (`Faction.neutral`; `Battle.fighting()`, `Battle.victor()`): they act and fight, but don't keep a battle going: it ends once one other side is left.
- [x] **Hunting** (`Unit.hunts`, `AI.HUNT`): the AI wants targets of a hunted side over others, by HUNT tiles' worth.
- [x] **Recovering AI** (`behaviour` recovering; `AI._recover()`).
- [x] **Inherent skills** (`Skill.inherent`): can't be disabled (`Unit.disable()`, `Unit.disable_order()`), removed, or put on the hotbar.
- [x] **Last stands** (`Battle.last_stand()`): d20 + average stat mod (`Unit.average_mod()`) + its best defensive bracing over its stats (`Unit.best_defensive_bonus()`); against an execute at rank r, × (HP left / max HP) / r (`Check.cut()`). Used by Level Down and Death.
- [x] **Level Down and Death** (`Skill.levels_down`, `Skill.kills`; `Battle._level_down()`, `Battle._death()`, `leveled`): see Warlock. Death's victims keep the HP they had (`Unit.slain_from`).
- [x] **Easy-to-remove effects** (`Effect.ease`; `Battle.cleanse()`, `Battle.dispel()`, `Battle._strip()`): stripping takes `ease` of their ranks for each spent.
- [x] **Smite by god** (`Battle._smite()`, `Battle._smite_heal()`, `Battle.disenchant()`): see Paladin. Bleeds can deal a set amount (`Effect.amount`, with no dice: Sol's Sunburn).
- [x] **Levels taken back** (`Unit.purchases`, `Unit.unranked()`): a level curse undoes the latest purchases past the unspent points.
- [x] **Tier gates** (`Skill.min_tier`; `Unit.missing()`).
- [x] **Allies** (`Faction.allies`, `Faction.friends()`, `Faction.sides_with_player`; `Battle.sides_left()`, `Battle.won()`): reads, attacks, watchers, stalls, sap and the battle's end treat allied sides as friends. **Companions** (`Battle.companions`, `Battle.from_role()`): the other starters, joining on an allied side.
- [x] **Potions refill** (`Item.empty`, `Item.emptied()`, `Item.filled()`; `Battle.refill()`): as the battle ends and as a starter is set up. Unequipping is free (`Battle.unequip()`); equipping costs the item's AP (`Battle.equip_ap()`).
- [x] **A ring's life** (`Item.revives`; `Unit.ring_lives()`, `Unit.over_max`; `Battle._revive()`): counted with a unit's lives, spent last, up at the ring's share of max HP (past 100%, more than full until it drops to full), with Resurrection Sickness (`sickness`; `Battle._mend()`).
- [x] **The player down** (`HUD.autopilot()`): while the player's unit is down or gone, its side plays itself, until the battle ends or it gets back up.
- [x] **Synergy** (`synergy` stat; `Battle.synergized()`, `Battle.read()`, `Battle._synergize()`): a party member and its leader, either with Synergy, share what they make out: each read is the clearer of the two (`Battle.clearer()`). As its side's turn ends, a unit gets its own first look at what a partner flagged: suspicious tiles (`inspect()`), and deceiving skills a partner saw through (`investigate()`), once each.
- [x] **General skills** (`Unit.GENERAL`, `Skill.min_tier`): on offer to every class (Synergy, tier 2).
- [x] **Chant** (`chant` stat; `Battle._chant()`, `chanted`; `Unit.chant_gains`, `Unit.chant_up`).
- [x] **Easier rolls** (`Skill.advantage`, `Skill.roll_per_rank`; `Battle.cast()`, `Battle.hit_chance()`) and ranges from a stat's score (`Skill.radius_stat`).
- [x] **Asphyxia** (`breathless` and `asphyxia` stats; `Battle._choke()`; `Unit.refit()` takes its share of max HP, refitting as effects tick, are stripped, or a unit gets up) and **Irradiate** (`irradiate`; the roll's overage, `Battle.cast()`).
- [x] **Gods by tab** (`SkillTab.god`; `Unit.deity()` goes by the unit's own god, then its devotion's, then its class's). A skill that needs a reserve needs it only from units that have it (`Unit.funding()`).
- [x] **Item AP by slot** (`Item.ap_cost(u)`, `Item.ARMOR_AP`; `Battle.item_ap()`).
- [x] **Command range for Synergy** (`Command.reach_of()`, `Command.party_reach_of()`; `Battle.synergized()`); `command` effects at `Effect.ENDLESS` or more read "no limit" (Quartz Ring).
