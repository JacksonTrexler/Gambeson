# Done: systems

`[x]` done · `(?N)` an open question in `docs/todo/` that may still change it · *Now:* what the game does today. Index: `completed.md`.

- [x] **Attack rolls.** d20 + attack mod against Defense for the skill's tags; a natural 20 hits and doubles damage; at least 1 damage before mitigation. The forecast shows the chance to hit when the player can identify the target, and the log shows each roll. A skill's `rolls` switch (in the editor) turns its roll off. (?5)
- [x] **Defense.** Armor sets it; `defense` effects add to it, untagged or tagged, with child tags counting (`physical` covers `pierce`). The unit card shows it ("11 · Gambeson", plus any tagged extras). Still to come: Sundered, and Attack Down (Interdict) on the attacker's side.
- [x] **Skill trees and ranks.** Roots (`SkillRoot`) of branches (`Branch`: the skills a branch comes after, or a count of its root's skills for capstones), a class's centre and core, and stubs for skills waiting on a question. Ranking up costs 1 + r², from the skills screen; a skill's `max_rank` can cap it. Tiers (`Role.tier()`) show on the skills screen. *Now:* only the Adventurer has a tree; other classes list their options, which can be ranked up too.
- [x] **Tabbed skill trees**: a tab per path (`SkillTab`: a centre, core and roots, like a class's own tree; `Role.tabs`). The skills screen shows a tab's wheel at a time, with a button per tab where the title was. A class's starting skills show on whichever tab has them. *Now:* the Rogue and Bard each have their own tab and **Calling: Freedom**.
- [x] **Energy recovery.** Rest, light activity and busy turns, as in the rule. A float shows what's regained.
- [x] **Dual-target skills**: buff allies, hurt enemies (starter spells). A skill's `dual` switch: allies get its effects, foes its damage, after an attack roll.
- [x] **Dragging** (a skill's `drags_to`; `Battle.drag_spot()`, `Battle.drag()`): a contest in place of the attack roll (`Battle.contested`, logged "(14 - 11)"); won, the foe goes onto the nearest free ground with the tag that the caster could walk to, and the caster in beside it. Drown. (?14)
- [x] **Grapples** (a skill's `grip`; `Battle._break_free()`, `Battle._grip()`): the foe is held (`grappled`) until it breaks free. Moving away first costs 1 + the holder's mod in the grip's stat tiles of movement and a contest in that stat (the holder keeps it on a tie); won, it walks on with what's left. Each turn it ends held, it takes the grip's dice + the holder's mod. A holder that falls or leaves its side lets go. Drown: 1d12 + str mod.
- [x] **Investigation**: enemies that can see a unit use a deceptive skill (Hide, Clever Disguise) check it at once, at advantage when close, against the one roll it made (kept on its effects; less its dissonance). Those who pass see through it. A skill's `investigated` switch turns this on.
- [x] **Seeing rolls** (Self-evaluation): a roll an ally's Self-evaluation reaches tallies up above the unit, like the initiative dice, and labels its effect's icon on the card. (?20)
- [x] **Standing still**: what counts as not having moved (resting, concealment that lasts until a unit moves, skills). Meander stretches it to rank tiles a turn. A skill's `until_move` switch makes its effects last until the unit moves.
- [x] **Fading effects**: hold for some turns, then lose a level a turn (a skill's `fades` switch: Clever Disguise).
- [x] **Per-skill rank costs**: a skill can scale its rank costs its own way (`rank_cost`, `rank_growth`: Clever Disguise).
- [x] **Self-only skills** (`self_only`: Hide, Clever Disguise): used from the hotbar at once, on the unit itself.
- [x] **Automatic skills** (a skill's `auto`; `Battle.auto_targets()`): used from the hotbar at once, landing on the nearest units in range the caster can place, allies and foes alike, no more than its rank; paid once. Sing.
- [x] **Dissonance and insight** (see Persona and insight): `Unit.dissonance()`, a point for each thing its persona puts forward at its rank (each vibe word; its stance and a claimed level; each identity tag and its name), one each for Embellish and Brood, plus `extra_dissonance` (Unit → Persona). The read sits on the unit card under its rows. Dissonance comes off the unit's own deceiving checks (`Unit.dissonance_penalty()`): the roll a deceiving skill makes, and the check it makes against units looking harder at it. A skill carrying its look cuts that by its rank (the Siren's Alluring IV: 7 − 4 = 3); insight still reads the full tally.
- [x] **Persona looks** (a skill's Look group; `Unit._take_looks()`): a stance, vibe words, tags and name pools a skill puts forward on whoever has it, on top of the unit's own, as the unit sets up. Alluring.
- [x] **Perceived stats**: the unit card shows a unit's **BST** (its six core stats added up) when its stats can be made out (net 1). Embellish inflates it and Brood deflates it, for those they fool.
- [x] **Fronting without trickery**: persona, Embellish and Brood fool allies that aren't insightful (`Perception.fronts_to()`). Allies fooled, by fronting or trickery, see the presentation, never the twisted fields.
- [x] **Stances** (the wrong team): `stance` on the unit (Unit → Persona), shown to those it fools from Persona III (Friendly from II). Reads carry it (`Read.stance`), and `Read.attitude()` says how the reader takes it: ally, foe, avoid or ignore. The AI strikes only foes, keeps a tile clear of the aggressive, and supports only allies. The unit card shows Aggressive, Passive or Traitor in place of Friendly or Enemy. Chosen in play with Personify's modal.
- [x] **Teammates**: the leader orders, and takes into its party, only units that it and they each take for a teammate (`Battle.teammates()`); a Traitor pretends to.
- [x] **Traitors in the enemy's party** (`Battle.posing()`): a Traitor that fools a leader can join its party. It acts on the leader's turn too, on whatever AP its own turn left it, and the player orders it like any party member (it shows only as the side reads it). Told to turn a hostile skill on its real allies, it refuses, or with a positive cha mod misses on purpose; either way the leader looks harder at it (`Battle.doubt()`: at advantage for a refusal, at disadvantage for a whiff). Not resting on its own side's turn counts as a refusal (`Battle.refused`, logged). Not ordered, it keeps beside its leader; on its own turn it rests unless it can make a kill (`AI._keep_up()`). Seen through, it leaves the party as the leader's next turn starts.
- [x] **Reading allies** by wis mod (`Perception._dim()`): −1 to −3, no details but healthy or bloodied and a word or two (`Perception.traits()`); −4 or less, only its side and what it looks like (`Role.noun`). Persona advertising (HP and level from II, vibe from I, identity from III) still shows. A unit's own concealment doesn't hide it from its allies.
- [x] **No cheating**: the unit card and tooltips show everyone but the leader as the player's side reads them (`HUD.view()`: the clearest read of its other units), its own allies included. They still show AP and movement while taking orders. An ally's skills show by name only (`Read.kit`) until net 3, and as "?" to a leader of negative wis mod, which can't order them used (`HUD.skill_sense()`). Its effects show as a "?" at net 1, in full at net 3.
- [x] **Gullible**: wis mod −3 or less falls for any front, an enemy's too, without trickery (`Perception.fronts_to()`).
- [x] **Advertised level** (`shows_level`, Persona II): its own, or one it claims to those it fools.
- [x] **Ramut's Ire** (`Battle._check_stall()`, `Skills/ramuts_ire.tres`, the `ire` stat): a side's turn is stalled when none of its units makes out a foe it would fight while enemies stand; the foes hiding or posing stall it. A turn that isn't stalled starts that side's count over. Once more turns in a row have stalled than the grace (`Battle.grace()`: the units remaining on the smallest side, × `ire_grace` (< 0: never), less what the side's prayers to Ramut took off), the Ire builds a rank on each of them every stalled turn, logged. Each rank defeats a rank of its concealment and trickery, against everyone, for the rest of the battle.
- [x] **Forced movement**, a pull (`Battle.pull()`, a skill's `pull`): Sing. Dragging is above; pushes are still to come.
- [x] **Craven** (`behaviour` on the unit): won't advance without an ally it trusts within 3 tiles whose level it reads as higher (`AI.CRAVEN_REACH`). Backs away from units it takes for Aggressive within that reach (`AI._flee()`).
- [x] **Lure** (`behaviour`: the Siren): serene and still until unmasked (`AI._luring()`). What comes into its reach it drowns, then rakes (`AI._ambush()`), leaving what it already holds to its grip; with nothing in reach, it sings.
- [x] **Healing** (a skill's Heal group; `Battle.mend()`): HP from the caster's mods, wounds cleared up to the heal's rank (`Effect.wound`; Renew: rank × rank), AP back (Renew), and per_wound × rank more for each wound cleared (First Aid). Regen clears wounds up to its own rank as it heals. Traps can leave wounds (`Terrain.wounds`: Snare's Bleeding, the `bleed` stat: dice + the setter's mod as each of the afflicted unit's side's turns ends, except the turn it was put on).
- [x] **Healer behaviours** (`AI._heal()`, `AI.want()`): how a unit tends (`Unit.tends`, else `Role.tends`): benevolent (the default; the largest share of HP missing), pragmatic (the highest level it reads, unless over half the heal would go to waste), loyal (its party first: `Unit.follows`, set by joining a party). Seen wounds it can clear count; commanders (the player's leader, or one with a party) a little more.
- [x] **Suspicion** (`Unit.suspicion`, `Battle.suspect()`, `Battle.doubt()`): stacks a unit builds on an enemy or a tile, each defeating a rank of its trickery and concealment (a tile's: what hides there) for that unit alone; any suspicion of a unit breaks its front. Units an enemy they can place is fooling look harder as their turn starts, and when it damages them with a hostile skill. Shown as a **Su** icon on the card, where the side makes out effects.
- [x] **Evidence** (`Battle._scatter()`, `Battle.debris`): using a skill that puts concealment on a unit (Hide) scatters debris from its investigated roll (`LITTER`): 15 or more (`CLEAN_HIDE`) leaves 1–2, 2–4 tiles off; 10–14 (`FAIR_HIDE`) 2–3, 1–3 off; less, 3–4 within 2 tiles. Moving while still concealed scatters one fewer around the path (not on it), by the roll its concealment was put on with, else d20 + dex mod (`Battle._hide_roll()`). Stepping on debris destroys it; `Battle._tidy_debris()` (after moves, casts and each side's turn) clears a revealed hider's (no concealment left, or fallen) and any whose hider is further than its band's far edge + dex mod. Kinds by the tile's tags, then `Battle.area_tags` (`DEBRIS_KINDS`); tiles with none (water) get none. Terrain tags added for it: grass (Grass, and the Spike Trap that looks like it), shrubbery (Bramble), stone (Rough, Wet Rock, Shrine); these material tags aren't listed in the tile panel. Enemies that make out the ground see it (`Battle.evidence()`; drawn as scattered strokes, with a tile-panel line), and the AI's Observe counts it as a clue.
- [x] **Hard to read** (`Perception._dim()`): a reader's wis mod for an ally's read is less the ally's cha mod when it's negative (a positive one adds nothing). Villagers (wis 12, cha 8) still read each other in full.
- [x] **Inspecting** (`Battle.inspect()`): a suspicious tile is one where a unit makes out something but not what (a hint of a hidden trap or unit), or one it already suspects and still can't make out. Each unit inspects the nearest one it hasn't before as its turn starts and after it moves. As its side's turn ends it looks around (`Battle._watch()`, `Battle.survey()`): the nearest within max(wis mod, 1) + rested tiles, 1 + rested of them, at +rested (rested: its turns in a row resting, up to its wis); with wis mod 0 or less only once every (1 − wis mod) turns; Eyes Forward, when it fires, replaces this look. Investigation skills search every tile in their area instead (`Battle.search()`). A tile where nothing hides gets no roll. Each check is d20 + wis mod + bonus − the distance penalty (`Battle.distance_penalty()`: none for adjacent tiles, else the tiles from the centre), against d20 + the concealment there. Passing grows a stack on the tile, and on a hidden enemy standing there. Logged for the player's units.
- [x] **Resisted skills** (a skill's `resisted`; `Battle._lured()`): only an enraptured foe (convinced the caster is its ally, with no suspicion of it) is pulled. On a suspicious one, the caster's stat against its `resisted` stat takes a stack of suspicion off. Sing: cha against wis.
- [x] **Grappler AI** (`AI._rooted()`): holding a unit, a unit won't walk off and let go, unless it reads its own tile as bad ground.
- [x] **Log grammar** (see Design rules): skill uses, rolls, investigations, suspicion, grips, refusals, traps and the Ire follow it.
