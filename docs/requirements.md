# Requirements

- **A unit's behaviour should be inferable from the unit itself.** A player should be able to guess what a unit might do by looking at it: does it look prideful, pitiful, pragmatic, capable? That reads as plain targeting, going for downs, squaring up, hedging. (The user, docs/agents/answers-2026-11-22.md: a rule of thumb, not a spec; no concrete AI behaviour has been specified yet.)
- Player units, and those they command, have behaviour too. It matters if autobattle is ever built, and when player control is stripped by cognitive disruption (a Disturbed unit cannot be commanded and acts on its behaviour alone).
